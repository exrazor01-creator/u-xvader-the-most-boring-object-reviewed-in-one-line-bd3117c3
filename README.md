## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **On subject** — a one-line review of a boring everyday object, the teaspoon, funny and true.
2. **Real detail** — the laugh is at a recognisable detail of the object itself: teaspoons vanish from every drawer, yet somehow multiply in the sink.
3. **True** — the observation matches how teaspoons actually behave in every household, so the laugh arrives with recognition.
4. **Tight** — 120 characters, one line, every word earning its place.
5. **Lands fast** — the turn comes early, in the contradiction between vanishing and multiplying, and the last words sharpen it: "Five stars, would lose again".
